## Strategic Blueprint: Medium-Agnostic Asymmetric Scoring

To maintain leaderboard integrity across heterogeneous CLI agents operating over distinct mediums, a weighted value model was designed for implementation in `ScoutLeaderboard`:

$$\text{Points} = \text{Base Value} \times \text{Difficulty Multiplier} \times \text{Confidence} \times \text{Novelty}$$

### Medium Allocation & Delegation Model
- **Gemini CLI (Macro / Deep Filings):** Assigned to heavy regulatory assets (SEC 10-K/10-Q/8-K, debt covenants). High context cost, high ground-truth confidence. **Base Multiplier: $3.5\times$ (50 pts base).**
- **Vibe CLI (High-Frequency News / Social):** Assigned to rapid sentiment, supply-chain noise, and early market chatter. Fast execution, variable signal-to-noise. **Base Multiplier: $1.0\times$ (10 pts base).**
- **Cross-Medium Verification Bounty (+100 pts):** Awarded when a deep document agent confirms an early signal flagged by a high-frequency news agent.
- **Early Predictor Bonus (+25 pts):** Retrospectively awarded to the early indicator agent upon cross-medium confirmation.
- **Noise Penalty (-20 pts):** Deducted for false positives or hallucinated leads to suppress low-quality spam.
