# ABOUT_EVIDENCE — proofs and boundaries for `ABOUT.md`

`ABOUT.md` is the one-paragraph offer fed to the drafting model. This file is the
review ledger for that paragraph; it is not prompt text.

## Claims permitted in the offer

| Claim | Evidence / boundary |
|---|---|
| Small teams can receive bounded Python/backend/AI automation cleanup | The offer is a proposed service, not a claim about a client result. The model may describe the scope, never a past outcome. |
| The work can include diagnosis, prioritized fixes, tests, or a small patch | This is the deliverable definition. It must be scoped and reviewed by the operator before acceptance. |
| Foxwood's own tooling uses path confinement, separate file/shell approvals, and append-only records | Verified in the MOLT/Whorl test suites and documented project artifacts. These are capabilities of the operator's tooling, not claims about a prospect's system. |
| Work is remote, fixed-scope, and not a retainer; a project-based bridge may be possible while a team hires | Current offer terms chosen by the operator. The bridge is an option to discuss, not a promise that every full-time post is a fit. No price or turnaround promise is made. |

## The model may not claim

- No years of experience, clients, employers, revenue, testimonials, or outcomes.
- No "we", team, enterprise, production-grade, battle-tested, or invented novelty claims.
- No familiarity with a prospect's code beyond that prospect's own post.
- No promise that a security issue will be fixed, only that it can be reviewed and a plan or small patch may be proposed.
- No price, timeline, availability, credentials, or technology experience not stated in `ABOUT.md`.
- Do not imply that a full-time hiring post has requested a contractor; frame any bridge as an optional question.
- No promise to contact, post, or send anything automatically.
- No use of ScholarScout, ScholarScout-derived artifacts, or quarantined material as Foxwood evidence.

## Operator-only fields

Before sending any draft, the operator must decide whether to add a real name,
rate, timezone, portfolio link, payment terms, and availability. The model must
not invent those details.
